04 / The process

Make the decisions visible.
Then prove the build.

A practical sequence keeps design choices, technical work, and launch evidence connected.

  1. 01

    Scope

    Define who the site is for, what it must say, and what it must let people do.

  2. 02

    Shape

    Set the content hierarchy and visual direction before expanding the system.

  3. 03

    Build

    Develop the responsive pages and only the integrations the project actually needs.

  4. 04

    Verify

    Test links, keyboard paths, narrow screens, reduced motion, assets, and browser errors.

  5. 05

    Launch & handoff

    Deploy through the agreed source path, read the live result back, and retain a rollback route.
